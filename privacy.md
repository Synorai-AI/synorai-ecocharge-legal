# Synorai EcoCharge Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 11, 2026_

Synorai EcoCharge ("the App") automatically applies Canadian environmental handling fees (EHF) for eligible electronics — in the online cart, at checkout, and on POS — and provides remittance reporting for all nine provinces with a regulated program.

## Data Collection

The App does not collect or store customer personal data for advertising or marketing purposes.

The App may access limited Shopify store data only to provide its core functionality, including:

- Product tags (to determine fee categories)
- Merchant-selected compliance province settings
- Cart line item information (to apply fee adjustments)
- Order information for remittance reporting (see below)

## Order Data and Remittance Reporting

To provide per-province EHF remittance reports, the App processes each paid order's shipping destination. The shipping address is read only to determine the destination **province and country code**.

Stored per order: destination province/country code, order number and date, and the environmental fee amounts charged and owed.

**Not stored, ever:** customer names, street addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifying customer information.

## Data Retention and Deletion

- Remittance records are kept only while the App is installed on a store.
- All of a store's records are deleted automatically on uninstall, and again upon Shopify's `shop/redact` webhook.
- Customer-level redaction requests are honored automatically; the App stores no customer-level personal data, so there is nothing to return or erase for an individual customer.

## Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest (managed, encrypted database and backups). Access is limited to the App's owner-operator via credentialed, logged accounts.

## Data Sharing

Synorai EcoCharge does not sell or share merchant or customer data with third parties. The only service provider is Render (cloud hosting), which hosts the application and database.

## Merchant Responsibility

Merchants remain responsible for all environmental fee compliance, reporting, and remittance obligations.
