Synorai EcoCharge Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Synorai EcoCharge automatically applies Canadian environmental handling fees (EHF) for eligible electronics — in the online cart, at checkout, and on POS — and provides remittance reporting for all nine provinces with a regulated program (AB, BC, MB, NB, NL, NS, PE, QC, SK).

Information we collect

To operate the app, we may access or process limited information from Shopify, including:

  • Store and installation information, such as the shop domain and app installation session data
  • App billing and subscription status needed to determine available app modes and features
  • Product and variant information, including product tags used for fee category logic
  • Store configuration data, including the merchant-selected province and app setup settings
  • Limited cart and merchandise data required to calculate or display supported environmental handling fees
  • Order information used for remittance reporting, described in detail below
  • Technical logs and webhook metadata needed for security, debugging, and support

Order data and remittance reporting

To provide per-province EHF remittance reports, the app processes each paid order’s shipping destination. We read the shipping address only to determine the destination province and country code.

What we store per order:

  • Destination province and country code (for example, “BC, CA”)
  • The order number and date
  • The environmental fee amounts charged and the amounts owed under the destination province’s schedule

We do not store customer names, street addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifying customer information.

Information provided directly by merchants

Merchants may provide information directly when installing the app, authenticating, changing settings, selecting a supported province, or contacting us for support.

How we use information

We use collected information to:

  • Authenticate the app and maintain secure app sessions
  • Save merchant settings and supported province configuration
  • Calculate, apply, and report supported environmental handling fees
  • Produce per-province EHF remittance reports for the merchant
  • Determine eligibility for Standard or Pro app behavior
  • Maintain app security, troubleshoot issues, and provide merchant support
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We use personal data only for the purposes described here, and never for advertising, marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making about individuals.

Data sharing

We do not sell merchant or customer data. We do not use merchant or customer data for advertising. Our only service provider is Render (cloud hosting), which hosts the application and its database.

Data retention and deletion

  • Remittance records are kept only while the app is installed on a store.
  • All of a store’s records are deleted automatically when the app is uninstalled, and again upon receipt of Shopify’s shop/redact webhook.
  • Customer-level redaction requests (customers/redact, customers/data_request) are honored automatically; because the app stores no customer-level personal data, there is nothing to return or erase for an individual customer.

Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest (managed, encrypted database and backups).
  • Access is limited to the app’s owner-operator via credentialed, logged accounts.
  • A security incident response policy is maintained; merchants are notified promptly of any incident affecting their data.

Merchant responsibility

Synorai EcoCharge automates fee application and reporting to assist with compliance. Merchants remain responsible for confirming their own legal, regulatory, compliance, reporting, and remittance obligations.
